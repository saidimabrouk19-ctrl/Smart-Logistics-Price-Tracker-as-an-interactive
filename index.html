Cabotage control (TMS)

Each van is limited to 3 cabotage operations within 7 days after unloading an international delivery. At 3/3 the van is locked automatically.

Rule used: EU Regulation 1072/2009 as a general limit (3 operations in 7 days, per vehicle, after an international delivery). Rules differ by country and vehicle type, so confirm with your compliance adviser before relying on this tool.
